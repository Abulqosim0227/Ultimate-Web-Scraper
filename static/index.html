How to use

Collect web pages into your own database, then ask questions. Answers come only from what you collected.

1Start the app

  • Run start.bat in the rag_app folder and keep its window open.
  • Open http://127.0.0.1:8000. Both dots at the bottom of the sidebar should be green.

2Collect pages with the RAG Spider

Install once in Chrome: open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose C:\Users\Admin\Desktop\rag-reference-full\rag_app\spider_extension. Pin it from the puzzle icon.

  • Turn it on: click the spider icon. The badge shows ON and the spider appears.
  • Open any page: the whole page is saved to your database automatically. Menus, footers, ads, share buttons and tags are skipped; on news sites only the article is kept. Wait until the panel says saved to your database before asking about it.
  • Move the mouse: the spider follows it and highlights the words it walks over. Stop moving and it keeps reading the page by itself. Scroll and it reads the new text.
  • Popups are read and saved as their own entries.
  • Come back later: the highlights return and the panel says already in your database. A page is never saved twice.
  • Internal sites work too, because the browser sends the text it sees.
  • Hide it: click the icon again or the × on the panel.

3Crawl a whole website

  • In the spider panel choose a limit (100 to 2000 pages) and press Crawl whole site.
  • It follows links on the same website, respects the site's robots.txt and takes one page per second. Press Stop any time.
  • Only public websites can be crawled. Internal addresses are refused for safety; open those pages with the spider instead.

4Ask questions

  • Type in the chat. Every answer cites its sources as numbers; click a number to see the page.
  • If your pages don't contain the answer you get No answer in your documents instead of a guess.
  • Tips: name the person, company or topic; ask in the language of the source for the most exact wording; each question is answered on its own, so repeat the subject in follow-ups.

5Your data

  • The sidebar shows how many pages and text chunks are saved.
  • Clean up all data deletes everything saved after you type DELETE. The tables stay. Chats in this browser and spider highlights are not affected.

?If something doesn't work

  • A red dot means PostgreSQL or Ollama is not running.
  • The panel says RAG app is not running: start the app first.
  • After the extension is updated: reload it in chrome://extensions, then press F5 on the page.
